# Product brief: create-a-comprehensive-ecommerce-app

Build a comprehensive mobile shopping app that lets our retail customers browse, discover, and buy our products easily on their phones, so we can grow sales beyond the store and keep shoppers coming back with a smooth, trustworthy buying experience.

## Target users
- Busy shoppers who prefer buying on mobile rather than visiting the store
- Existing in-store customers who want to reorder familiar products from home
- New customers discovering our brand through search, social, or referrals
- Gift buyers looking for curated products with reliable delivery
- Returning loyal customers who expect order history, saved items, and faster checkout

## Business goals
- Increase online revenue and reduce dependence on walk-in traffic alone
- Make it simple for customers to find products, check stock, and complete a purchase in a few taps
- Grow repeat purchases through accounts, order history, and personalized recommendations
- Present our catalog professionally so the brand feels trustworthy on mobile
- Capture customer data (with consent) to improve merchandising and marketing
- Support scalable growth as the catalog and order volume expand

## Key features
- Product catalog with categories, search, filters, and product detail pages (photos, price, description, variants)
- Shopping cart and secure checkout with multiple payment options
- User accounts with sign-up/sign-in, profile, and saved addresses
- Order placement, order history, and basic order status tracking
- Wishlist / saved items for later purchase
- Push notifications for order updates and selected promotions
- Home screen with featured products, promotions, and bestsellers
- Inventory-aware browsing so customers see availability clearly
- Customer support contact path (in-app help or message link)
- Admin-ready product and order operations so the business can manage catalog and fulfill orders

## Constraints
- Must work well on mainstream iOS and Android smartphones
- Must feel simple for non-technical shoppers; no complicated flows
- Must protect customer payment and personal data with industry-standard security practices
- Must support a realistic retail catalog (hundreds of SKUs initially, growing over time)
- Launch should prioritize core shopping and checkout before advanced extras
- Content and pricing must be maintainable by the business without heavy technical work day-to-day
- Budget and timeline should favor a practical MVP first, then iterative improvements

## Open questions
- Exact product categories and initial catalog size for launch
- Whether we sell only our own brand, third-party brands, or a mix
- Primary markets / countries and required currencies and languages at launch
- Preferred payment providers and whether we need cash-on-delivery or buy-now-pay-later
- Shipping model: own delivery, courier partners, click-and-collect, or all of these
- Return and refund policy details customers will see in the app
- Whether loyalty points, coupons, and referral rewards are required for MVP or phase 2
- Brand name, visual identity, and tone for the customer-facing app (beyond the working project name)
- Whether guest checkout is allowed or account creation is required
- Integration needs with existing POS, inventory, or accounting systems
