# Clarifications

Q: For MVP, is account sign-in required for browsing and checkout, or must guest checkout be supported?
A: Browsing the catalog does not require sign-in. Guest checkout must be supported for MVP so shoppers can buy in a few taps without creating an account first. Account sign-up/sign-in remains available and is encouraged for order history, saved addresses, wishlist, and faster repeat checkout.
Q: Which primary market(s), currency(ies) (ISO 4217), and language(s) must launch support?
A: Primary launch market is the United States. Currency: USD (ISO 4217). Language: English only for MVP. Additional markets, currencies, and languages can follow after the core shopping experience is proven.
Q: Is payment limited to Stripe card PaymentIntents (test mode) for MVP, or are cash-on-delivery and/or buy-now-pay-later required at launch?
A: MVP payment is limited to Stripe card payments via PaymentIntents (test mode for development/launch validation, then live mode for real charges). Cash-on-delivery and buy-now-pay-later are not required at launch; they can be considered in a later phase.
Q: Which fulfillment model(s) are in MVP scope: own delivery, courier partners, click-and-collect, or a combination—and what delivery fee / ETA rules apply?
A: MVP fulfillment is courier partner shipping plus click-and-collect from our store. Own same-day fleet delivery is out of scope for MVP. Delivery fee rules: free standard shipping on orders of $50 USD or more; otherwise a flat $5.99 shipping fee. Standard delivery ETA shown to customers is 3–5 business days. Click-and-collect is free, with ready-for-pickup ETA of 2–4 hours during store hours (next business day if ordered near closing).
Q: What return and refund rules must the app show and enforce (window, eligible conditions, refund vs exchange, who initiates)?
A: Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery for unused items in original condition with tags/packaging where applicable. Final-sale items (clearance, personal-care opened, perishable, or explicitly marked non-returnable) are not eligible. Prefer refund to original payment method within about 5–10 business days after we receive and approve the return; exchange for size/color variant is offered when stock allows. The customer initiates the return or exchange from order history (or via in-app support); store/ops staff approve and process fulfillment of the refund or exchange.
Q: Are coupons, loyalty points, and referral rewards required in MVP, or deferred to a later phase?
A: Coupons, loyalty points, and referral rewards are deferred to a later phase. MVP focuses on catalog, cart, secure checkout, accounts, order history/status, wishlist, and basic promotions messaging (e.g., featured deals on the home screen) without a full rewards engine.
Q: Do we sell only our own brand, third-party brands, or a mix—and does that change merchandising or seller/admin roles for MVP?
A: We sell our own retail brand only for MVP—no multi-vendor marketplace. Merchandising is a single owned catalog (categories, featured products, bestsellers). Admin roles stay simple: our staff manage products, pricing, stock, and orders. Third-party brands or seller portals are not in MVP scope.
Q: Must MVP integrate with an existing POS, inventory, or accounting system, or is in-app/admin catalog and stock management sufficient at launch?
A: No POS, inventory, or accounting system integration is required for MVP. In-app/admin catalog and stock management is sufficient at launch so we can maintain products, prices, and inventory without heavy technical work day-to-day. Integrations can be added later as volume grows.
Q: Who are the admin/ops roles for catalog and order fulfillment in MVP (e.g., single admin vs separate merchandiser and fulfillment operator), and what can each do?
A: MVP uses two practical roles: (1) Merchandiser/Admin — manage catalog (add/edit products, photos, prices, variants, categories), set featured/promo placements, adjust stock levels, and view orders; (2) Fulfillment Operator — view and update order status (paid, packing, shipped/ready for pickup, completed, cancelled), mark click-and-collect ready, enter tracking when shipping via courier, and process approved returns/refunds. One person may hold both roles early on, but permissions should support separating them as the team grows.
