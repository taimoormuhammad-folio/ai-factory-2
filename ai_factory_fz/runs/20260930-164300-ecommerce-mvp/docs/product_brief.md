# Product brief: ShopEase

Our own fast, simple and easy-to-use mobile shopping app for Android and iOS where customers can discover our clothing and accessories, buy them in a few taps, and follow their orders. We want a first version live quickly, then grow it step by step based on what customers actually do.

## Target users
- Existing customers of our clothing and accessories who want a quicker, more convenient way to buy from us on their phone
- New shoppers aged roughly 18-45 who discover our brand and prefer to shop on mobile rather than on a desktop website
- Repeat buyers who want to reorder, check past orders and see the status of each order
- Our internal store staff/admins who add products, manage stock and prices, and process orders (needed behind the scenes even though they are not shoppers)

## Business goals
- Launch a first working version (MVP) quickly on both Android and iOS, then grow it in later releases
- Give customers a direct sales channel we own instead of depending only on marketplaces and third-party platforms
- Increase online sales and make the mobile checkout as smooth as possible so fewer customers abandon their carts
- Encourage repeat purchases by making it easy to create an account, see order history and come back
- Keep the app simple, fast and easy to use so that customers need no explanation
- Learn from real usage (what people search for, view, add to cart and buy) to decide what to build next

## Key features
- Account creation and sign-in with email and password (simple and reliable for version 1), including password reset
- Browse products by category (e.g. Women, Men, Accessories) and search by keyword
- Product detail page with multiple photos, description, price, available sizes and colors, and clear indication of what is out of stock
- Shopping cart: add products with chosen size and color, change quantities, remove items, and see the running total
- Checkout with delivery address entry and payment by card through a trusted third-party payment provider (we will not store card details ourselves), with an order confirmation shown and sent by email
- Order history: list of past orders with an order detail page and a clear status for each order (e.g. Placed, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled)
- Basic admin back-office (web-based) so our team can manage products, photos, prices, stock and update order statuses
- Fast loading and a clean, minimal interface that works well on common phone sizes

## Constraints
- Must work on both Android and iOS from the first release; we prefer a single shared codebase approach to save time and cost, and leave that technical choice to the development team
- Speed to market matters more than a long feature list, so version 1 stays limited to the features listed above
- Card payments must be handled by a certified payment provider so we do not carry the burden of storing card data; payments and personal data must be secure and comply with applicable privacy and data protection rules (e.g. GDPR)
- Product catalog is modest at launch (a few hundred products) and must be manageable by non-technical staff
- The app must connect with our existing product and stock information so items shown are actually available; a simple manual or spreadsheet-based import is acceptable for launch
- Budget is limited and should be spent on the core shopping journey rather than extras; no promised fixed figure yet, but we expect a phased plan
- Version 1 is in a single language and a single currency, selling to our home market only

## Open questions
- Which countries or regions will we ship to at launch, and which currency and language should the app use?
- Which payment provider do we want to use, and should we also add Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal soon after launch?
- Should customers be able to check out as a guest without creating an account?
- Do we want social sign-in (Google, Apple) in addition to email and password? Apple may require Sign in with Apple if other social logins are offered.
- What delivery options, shipping prices and estimated delivery times do we offer, and do we want free shipping above a certain order value?
- What is our returns and refund policy, and should customers be able to start a return inside the app?
- Do we need push notifications (e.g. order shipped) in version 1, or is email enough at first?
- Should we add a wishlist/favorites, discount codes, or a loyalty program in a second release, and in what order?
- How will stock be kept in sync with our physical shop(s) and current systems, and how often?
- How will we handle taxes/VAT display on prices and invoices?
- What is the target launch date and the budget ceiling for version 1?
- Who on our team will own product content (photos, descriptions) and customer support after launch?
- Which success numbers matter most in the first three months (e.g. downloads, conversion rate, repeat purchase rate)?
