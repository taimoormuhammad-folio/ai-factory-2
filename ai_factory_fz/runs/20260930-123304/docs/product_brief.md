# Product brief: Simple Shop Mobile App

A simple, fast mobile shopping app that lets our customers browse our products, buy in a few taps, and come back easily, extending our retail business onto customers' phones. The brief was only 'create a simple app', so I have decided it should be a shopping app for our retail business and kept it deliberately small at launch.

## Target users
- Existing in-store and online customers who already know our brand and want a faster way to reorder and buy
- New customers aged roughly 18-55 who discover us through social media or word of mouth and shop mainly on their phones
- Repeat buyers who want saved details, order history, and easy reordering
- Internal store staff who will manage products, prices, stock, and orders (back-office use, basic needs only)

## Business goals
- Launch a first usable version (MVP) within about 3-4 months so we can start taking orders from the app
- Give customers a simple and quick path from opening the app to paying for an order
- Drive repeat purchases, with a target of 25% of app customers ordering again within 60 days
- Reach around 10% of total online sales through the app within 6 months of launch
- Lower the cost of acquiring repeat sales by using push notifications and offers instead of paid advertising
- Keep the app simple so that support requests stay low and our small team can manage it

## Key features
- Account sign-up and login (email or phone, plus guest checkout to reduce drop-off)
- Product catalogue with categories, photos, prices, and descriptions
- Search and basic filters (category, price)
- Product detail page with stock availability
- Shopping cart and simple checkout
- Payments by card and common digital wallets (Apple Pay / Google Pay)
- Delivery address management and choice between home delivery and in-store pickup
- Order confirmation and order tracking with status updates
- Order history with a one-tap reorder button
- Push notifications for order updates and occasional promotions
- Wishlist or favourites
- Simple admin area for staff to manage products, prices, stock, and orders
- Basic sales reporting (orders, revenue, top products)

## Constraints
- Budget is limited, so launch scope is limited to the features listed and anything extra comes later
- Launch on both iPhone and Android, preferably using a single shared codebase to control cost
- Must connect to our existing product and stock data so we do not maintain inventory twice
- Must handle payments securely through a trusted payment provider; we will not store card details
- Must comply with privacy and data protection rules for customer data (e.g. GDPR or local equivalent)
- The app must be easy enough for non-technical customers and staff to use without training
- Needs to load quickly and work acceptably on average mobile connections
- Launch in one country and one language first

## Open questions
- Which country or countries and currencies do we launch in first?
- Which payment provider will we use, and what fees are acceptable?
- Do we integrate with our current stock/point-of-sale system at launch, or upload products manually at first?
- What are our delivery options, costs, and areas, and do we offer in-store pickup from every store?
- Do we want a loyalty or rewards programme in the first version or later?
- How will returns and refunds be handled in the app?
- What is the firm budget and launch date?
- Who will provide product photos and descriptions, and how many products will be in the catalogue at launch?
- What brand guidelines (logo, colours) should the app follow?
- What customer support channel should be in the app (chat, email, phone)?
- Is there a need for multiple languages or accessibility standards beyond the basics?
