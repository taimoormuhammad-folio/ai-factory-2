# Product brief: ShopEase

A simple, fast and easy-to-use mobile shopping app for Android and iOS where customers can browse our clothing and accessories and buy them in a few taps. We want a first version live quickly and then grow it over time.

## Target users
- Existing and new customers of our clothing and accessories store who shop on their phones
- Style-conscious shoppers, mainly ages 18-45, who are comfortable paying by card online
- Repeat customers who want to check past orders and their status without contacting us
- Internal store staff who fulfil orders and update order statuses (secondary user, needs a basic way to see and update orders)

## Business goals
- Launch a first version (MVP) quickly on both Android and iOS, then grow it in later releases
- Give customers our own direct sales channel so we depend less on third-party marketplaces
- Make buying easy so that customers can go from browsing to paid order with as few steps as possible
- Increase repeat purchases by letting customers keep an account and see their order history
- Reduce customer service load by showing each order's status in the app
- Build a base of registered customers that we can later market to (with consent)

## Key features
- Account creation and sign-in (email and password for the first version)
- Browse products by category (e.g. clothing and accessories, with subcategories) and search products
- Product details page with photos, available sizes and colors
- Shopping cart: add products, change quantities, remove items
- Checkout with card payment through a trusted third-party payment provider (we do not store card data ourselves)
- Order history with each order's status (e.g. placed, paid, shipped, delivered)
- Fast, simple, clean interface that is easy to use for non-technical shoppers

## Constraints
- Must work on both Android and iOS
- First version must go live quickly, so scope is limited to the features listed in the brief; anything else is for later releases
- Keep the app simple, fast and easy to use
- Card payments must be handled securely by a certified payment provider; we will not handle raw card data
- Product catalogue, prices and stock come from our existing product data; we need a straightforward way to keep them up to date
- We are a growing retail business with a limited budget and a small team, so we prefer proven off-the-shelf services over custom-built ones
- Sales are initially in a single country and currency

## Open questions
- Which country and currency do we launch in, and do we ship internationally later?
- Which payment provider will we use, and do we also want Apple Pay / Google Pay after the first version?
- What are our shipping options and costs, and how do we handle taxes?
- Do we allow guest checkout, or is an account required to buy?
- Do we add social sign-in (Google, Apple) later?
- How do returns and refunds work, and should they be requested in the app?
- How will stock levels be managed, and what should the app show when an item or size is out of stock?
- Who will maintain products and photos, and through which back-office tool?
- Do we want push notifications for order status changes, and from which release?
- Do we want wishlists, discount codes, loyalty points or reviews in a later release?
- What is the target launch date and budget for the first version?
- How will we measure success (for example number of orders, conversion rate, app downloads) and what targets do we set?
