# Clarifications

Q: Which country and currency will we launch in (single ISO 4217 currency), and is international shipping explicitly out of scope for the first release?
A: We launch in a single country with a single currency. My decision: United States, USD. International shipping is explicitly out of scope for the first release; we may look at it in a later release once domestic sales are stable.
Q: Shipping: what delivery options and costs apply (flat rate, free over a threshold, or per-option rates), and is a single shipping address per order sufficient with address book management in scope?
A: Keep it simple: one standard delivery option at a flat rate of $5.00, free for orders of $75 or more. No express option in the first release. A single shipping address per order is sufficient. Customers enter the address at checkout and it can be saved to their account for reuse, but a full multi-address book with management screens is out of scope. One saved default address that they can edit is enough.
Q: Taxes: should displayed prices include tax (e.g. VAT/sales tax), and do we need tax shown as a separate line on orders/receipts?
A: Product prices are displayed before sales tax, as is normal in the US. Sales tax is calculated at checkout using a flat rate configured for our home state, since we ship domestically from one location. Tax must be shown as a separate line in the cart summary, order details and receipt, alongside subtotal, shipping and total. Automated multi-state tax calculation is for a later release.
Q: Guest checkout: confirm that an account is required to add to cart and buy in the first release (guest carts out of scope).
A: Confirmed. An account is required to buy, and guest checkout is out of scope. Customers can browse and search without signing in, but they must sign in or register to add items to the cart and check out. This supports our goal of building a base of registered customers.
Q: Returns and refunds: for the first release, is the in-app flow out of scope (handled manually by staff via Stripe dashboard, with status 'refunded' set by staff), or do you need customers to request returns in the app?
A: The in-app returns flow is out of scope for the first release. Customers contact us by email, and staff handle returns and refunds manually via the Stripe dashboard. Staff then set the order status to 'refunded' in the staff tool. The app should show our return policy as simple text (30 days, unworn items with tags) and a support contact email.
Q: Stock display: when a size/color variant is out of stock, should it be shown greyed out and unselectable, hidden, or allow 'notify me'? Also, how long should stock be reserved during checkout before an unpaid payment expires (e.g. 15 or 30 minutes)?
A: Out-of-stock size/color variants should be shown greyed out and unselectable, so customers can see we normally carry them. If every variant is out of stock, show a clear 'Out of stock' label and disable Add to cart. 'Notify me' is out of scope for now. Stock is reserved for 30 minutes during checkout; if payment is not completed by then, the order expires, is cancelled and the stock is released.
Q: Catalog management: who maintains products, prices, stock and photos, and through what tool? Should the MVP include a simple admin/back-office (web) for catalog CRUD, or will data be imported from existing product data (CSV/API/feed) and on what frequency?
A: Our merchandising team (two people) will maintain the catalogue. We already have product data, so for the MVP we want to import it rather than build a full catalogue editor: a CSV import (products, variants, prices, stock, photo URLs) that can be uploaded through a simple web admin page or run as a scheduled import. Stock and prices should be refreshed at least once a day, and ideally we can trigger a manual re-import at any time. A full catalogue create/edit back-office is for a later release. Photos are hosted as image files and referenced by URL in the import.
Q: Staff order management: what exactly must staff be able to do (view list of orders, filter by status, mark as fulfilled and delivered, cancel, see customer shipping address)? Is this in the mobile app, a web admin, or both, and do staff need separate login roles?
A: Staff need a basic web admin (not in the customer mobile app) where they can: view the list of orders, filter by status, open an order to see items, customer name and shipping address, mark orders as shipped (fulfilled) and delivered, cancel an order, and mark an order as refunded after handling it in Stripe. Staff log in with separate staff accounts, distinct from customer accounts. Two roles are enough: staff and admin (admin can also manage staff accounts and trigger catalogue imports). Customers must never access this area.
Q: Order status terminology: the brief lists 'placed, paid, shipped, delivered'. Can we use our standard flow (pending_payment, paid, fulfilled, delivered, plus cancelled and refunded) and display 'fulfilled' to customers as 'Shipped'? Is tracking number/carrier info needed?
A: Yes, use your standard internal flow: pending_payment, paid, fulfilled, delivered, cancelled, refunded. Show customers friendly labels: 'Awaiting payment', 'Paid', 'Shipped' (for fulfilled), 'Delivered', 'Cancelled', 'Refunded'. A tracking number and carrier are not required for the first release, but staff should be able to optionally enter a tracking number when marking an order as shipped, and it should display in the order details if present. No carrier API integration.
Q: Notifications: are push notifications and order confirmation emails in or out of the first release? If emails are in, is a transactional email service acceptable and which events (order confirmation, status changes)?
A: Push notifications are out of scope for the first release and planned for a later one. Transactional emails are in, using an off-the-shelf transactional email service, which is fine. Events: order confirmation after successful payment, and a 'your order has shipped' email when status changes to shipped. Also password reset emails. Other status-change emails can wait.
Q: Account features: do you need password reset via email, email verification, and in-app account deletion in the first release (account deletion is required by our privacy conventions and app store rules)? What personal data do you want collected beyond email, name and shipping address?
A: Yes to all three. Password reset via email is in. Email verification is in (required before first checkout is acceptable, browsing and signing in can happen right after registration). In-app account deletion is in, as required by app store rules; past orders should be retained in anonymised form for our accounting records. We collect only email, name, password, shipping address and an optional phone number for delivery. No date of birth, gender or other personal data.
Q: Payment methods: confirm that card payments via Stripe (test mode for now) are the only payment method at launch and that Apple Pay / Google Pay are out of scope. Is a production Stripe account needed before launch or is test mode acceptable for the first release?
A: Confirmed: card payments via Stripe are the only payment method at launch, and Apple Pay / Google Pay are out of scope for the first release (we want them soon after). Test mode is fine during development and testing, but we need a live production Stripe account connected before public launch, since we want real sales. We will set up and verify the production account ourselves.
Q: Search and browsing: what level of search is needed (simple keyword match on product name/description, or also filters and sorting by size, color, price)? How large is the catalogue (approx. number of products and variants)?
A: Simple keyword search on product name and description is enough for the first release. Browsing by category and subcategory is in. For the product list, basic sorting by price (low to high, high to low) and newest is nice to have; filters by size, color and price range are for a later release. The catalogue is approximately 300 products with about 2,000 variants (size/color combinations), with 4 to 6 photos per product at most.
Q: Launch targets: what is the target launch date, minimum supported OS versions for Android and iOS, supported languages (single language?), and what success metrics and targets (orders, conversion rate, downloads) should the PRD state?
A: Target launch is within about 4 months, i.e. end of January 2027, ahead of the spring season, with a limited budget and a small team. Minimum OS versions: Android 10 and iOS 16 (to cover the vast majority of our customers). Single language: English. Success metrics for the first 3 months after launch: 5,000 app downloads, 1,500 registered accounts, 600 paid orders, a browse-to-paid-order conversion rate of at least 2%, 20% of orders from repeat customers, and a reduction in 'where is my order' support emails of at least 30%.
Q: Marketing consent: should registration include an optional marketing consent checkbox now to build the customer base for later marketing, or is this entirely out of scope for the first release?
A: Yes, include an optional marketing consent checkbox at registration, unchecked by default, with short clear wording and a link to the privacy policy. We store the consent status and the timestamp on the customer account so we can export consented customers later. We are not sending marketing messages in the first release; campaigns, and any in-app preference screen for changing consent, are for later.
