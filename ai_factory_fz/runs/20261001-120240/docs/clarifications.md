# Clarifications

Q: Which single country and currency launch first (e.g., US/USD)? This determines shipping zones, tax rules, legal copy, and price display.
A: Launch in the United States with USD ($) as the only currency for MVP. All prices, shipping zones, tax rules, and legal copy should be US-focused. We ship domestically within the US only at launch—no international orders in sprint one.
Q: What is the MVP shipping pricing model: flat-rate fee, free-shipping threshold, or calculated by weight/zone—and what is the default rate or threshold?
A: Use a simple flat-rate model for MVP: $5.99 standard shipping on all domestic US orders. Offer free shipping on orders of $75 or more before discounts. Do not build weight- or zone-based calculation in sprint one—we can revisit once order volume justifies it.
Q: What returns and refunds policy applies at launch (e.g., 14-day, 30-day, exchange-only), and must the app support initiating returns/refunds or display policy text only with manual processing offline?
A: 30-day returns on unworn items with original tags attached; refunds to the original payment method once we receive and inspect the return. Exchanges are handled as return plus new order for MVP. The app must display clear returns policy text on product pages, checkout, and order confirmation—but no in-app return initiation flow in sprint one. Customers contact us by email to start a return; we process everything manually offline.
Q: Which promo code types are in scope for MVP (percentage off, fixed amount, free shipping), and what rules apply (single-use vs multi-use, minimum order value, expiry)?
A: In scope for MVP: percentage-off codes (e.g., 10% off) and fixed-amount-off codes (e.g., $5 off). Free-shipping promo codes are out of scope for sprint one—we already have a free-shipping threshold at checkout. Support both single-use codes (e.g., welcome offer for one customer) and multi-use campaign codes (e.g., LAUNCH10 for early marketing). Optional minimum order value per code (e.g., $30 minimum). Codes must support an expiry date. Show the applied discount clearly in cart and checkout before payment.
Q: Are product reviews limited to verified purchasers only, and should reviews auto-publish or require manual approval before appearing on product pages?
A: Yes—reviews are limited to verified purchasers only (customers who bought that shirt through ShirtStack). Reviews auto-publish immediately after submission for MVP so the catalog feels alive for demos and early shoppers. We will handle moderation manually offline if a review is reported or clearly abusive; no admin approval queue in sprint one.
Q: Which authentication methods are required at launch: email/password only, or also Google and/or Apple sign-in?
A: Email and password are required at launch. Also include Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In on mobile—our shoppers are phone-first and we need fast checkout with minimal friction. Guest browsing stays as-is; account is required at checkout, and social sign-in counts toward that.
Q: How is inventory managed for MVP: real-time stock sync with warehouse/ERP, or manual stock counts updated on a schedule (e.g., daily)—and how should the app behave when an item goes out of stock during checkout?
A: Manual stock counts updated once daily by our team—no real-time ERP or warehouse sync in sprint one. At add-to-cart and again at checkout, validate stock against the latest count. If an item goes out of stock during checkout, block completion for that line item, show a clear out-of-stock message, and let the shopper remove it or reduce quantity. Do not charge for unavailable items; refresh cart totals automatically.
Q: Should sales tax/VAT be calculated and displayed at checkout by region for the launch market, or excluded from MVP with tax handled manually/offline?
A: Yes—calculate and display applicable US sales tax at checkout based on the customer's shipping address (state/zip). Use the payment provider's tax tooling (e.g., Stripe Tax) so totals are accurate and credible for investors and customers. Tax is not handled manually offline for MVP; it must appear in the order summary before payment and on the order confirmation.
