# Clarifications

Q: Guest checkout conflict: our project rules treat carts as belonging to signed-in users and put guest carts out of scope unless the PRD says otherwise, but the brief requires guest browsing and guest checkout as P0. Should the MVP include guest carts and guest checkout (and guest order tracking by email and order number), or require sign-in to add to cart or check out, with guests only browsing?
A: Please treat the brief as the PRD on this point: guest carts and guest checkout are in the MVP as P0. Guests can browse, add to cart and check out with just their email, shipping address and payment. The guest cart lives on the device. If the customer signs in or registers, it is merged into their account cart. Guests can track an order with email plus order number. After a guest order is placed, the confirmation screen and email offer a simple option to create an account with the same email, but it is optional.
Q: Payment methods conflict: our standard is Stripe PaymentIntents in test mode with a webhook marking orders paid, but the brief also requires cash on delivery and bank transfer. Is Stripe confirmed as the gateway? Should COD and bank transfer be in the MVP at launch? If so, how are they confirmed (for example, an admin manually marks a bank transfer as paid)?
A: Yes, Stripe is confirmed as the gateway, using PaymentIntents for credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex). Build and test in Stripe test mode, and switch to live keys at launch. The Stripe webhook marks card orders as paid. Apple Pay and Google Pay are not required for launch. Yes, COD and bank transfer are both in the MVP at launch because our customers ask for them. Both are confirmed manually by staff in the admin dashboard. For bank transfer, staff mark the order Paid after checking our bank account. For COD, staff mark the order Paid once the delivery partner confirms cash was collected.
Q: Order status conflict: the brief defines Pending → Confirmed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered, while our standard flow is pending_payment → paid → fulfilled → delivered, plus cancelled and refunded. Which set should the PRD use? If we keep the brief's statuses, how do they map to payment state (paid, unpaid, COD pending) and to cancelled and refunded?
A: Use the brief's statuses for what customers see: Pending → Confirmed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered. Add Cancelled as an admin-only end state. Keep a separate payment status on every order: Unpaid, Paid, COD Pending, Failed, Refunded, Partially Refunded. Mapping: Card orders are Pending/Unpaid until the Stripe webhook confirms payment, then become Confirmed/Paid automatically. Bank transfer orders are Pending/Unpaid until staff mark them paid, then Confirmed/Paid. COD orders become Confirmed straight away with payment status COD Pending, and move to Paid when cash is collected. Cancelled and refunded are not extra steps in the delivery flow: cancellation sets order status to Cancelled, and refunds are recorded in the payment status.
Q: Bank transfer: how long is an unpaid order held, with its stock reserved, before it is auto-cancelled? Who verifies the transfer and how?
A: We hold a bank transfer order for 48 hours with stock reserved. If payment is not marked received by then, the order is auto-cancelled and the stock is released. The customer sees our bank details and a unique payment reference (the order number) on the confirmation screen and in the email. Our store staff verify the transfer by checking our bank account for that reference and amount, then mark the order Paid in the admin dashboard. Staff can extend the hold manually for a specific order if needed.
Q: Cash on delivery: are there order value limits, excluded regions or extra fees? Who marks a COD order as paid, and when?
A: COD is available for orders up to £300. It is not available for remote regions (Highlands and islands, and any area outside our normal delivery zone). There is a flat COD fee of £3, shown at checkout and in the order total. Store staff mark a COD order as Paid in the admin dashboard once the delivery partner confirms cash was collected, usually when it is delivered or when the courier remits the cash.
Q: Stock reservation: should stock be reserved at checkout/order placement (our default, released on failed or expired payment) rather than when an item is added to the cart? Please confirm. What is the low-stock threshold, global or per product or variant?
A: Confirmed: reserve stock when the order is placed, not when an item is added to the cart. Release it if card payment fails or expires (about 30 minutes) or a bank transfer order expires after 48 hours. The cart shows current availability and blocks checkout for unavailable items. Low stock is a single global threshold of 5 units, applied per variant where variants exist. Per-product thresholds are not needed for the MVP.
Q: Launch market: which country, currency (ISO 4217 code) and tax rules apply? Are taxes added on top of prices or already included in them? Which regions are covered by delivery?
A: We launch in the United Kingdom only, with currency GBP and English only. Prices shown in the app already include VAT at 20%, and the cart and checkout show the VAT portion of the total. The VAT rate is configurable by us, but one standard rate is enough for launch. Delivery covers mainland England, Scotland and Wales. Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man and international addresses are not covered at launch. Remote areas such as the Highlands and islands are covered only at a higher flat shipping rate and without COD.
Q: Shipping: which shipping methods (standard, express, pickup) are offered? What are the flat rates or region/order-value rules, and what is the free-shipping threshold?
A: We offer two methods at launch: Standard (3-5 working days) and Express (next working day for orders placed before 2pm). There is no pickup in the MVP. Standard is £4.95 flat for mainland and £9.95 for remote regions. Express is £9.95 flat for mainland, and not offered to remote regions. Standard shipping is free for orders over £75 (mainland). Free shipping is also available through a free-shipping coupon or promotion. There is no real-time courier rate integration. Rules are simple and editable by admin.
Q: Cancellation, returns and refunds: who can cancel an order (customer or admin only) and up to which status? Are customer-initiated returns in scope, or is there only admin cancellation and refund? Are refunds full only, or partial as well? Are COD and bank-transfer refunds handled manually outside the app?
A: In the MVP only admin can cancel an order in the app. Customers ask us through the contact form or email, and we can cancel up to and including Processing. Once an order is Shipped it cannot be cancelled, and it goes through our returns policy instead. There is no customer-initiated return flow in the app. Our policy is 30 days for returns of unused items, handled by support and processed by admin. Refunds can be full or partial. Card refunds are issued by admin through Stripe from the dashboard. COD and bank-transfer refunds are handled manually outside the app (bank transfer back to the customer), and admin just records the refund and updates the payment status.
Q: Admin tools: is a web dashboard (current preference) confirmed for the MVP? Do we need multiple roles and permissions (product manager, order manager, support agent) or a single admin role?
A: Yes, a web dashboard is confirmed for the admin tools, and there is no admin inside the mobile app. For the MVP, a single admin role is enough, but each staff member has their own login so actions can be traced. Separate roles (product manager, order manager, support agent) are a later phase.
Q: Catalog scope: what is the approximate number of products, brands and variants at launch? Is spreadsheet bulk import required in the MVP? Which specifications are mandatory per category (for example IP rating for outdoor, bulb type for bulbs)?
A: At launch we expect about 150 products, 20 brands and roughly 400 variants in total. We need a spreadsheet (CSV) import for the initial catalog load, because entering this by hand is impractical. It can be a one-off import run for us at launch; an ongoing self-service import tool is not required. Mandatory specs for all products: wattage, light type, dimmable, material, finish, dimensions and weight. Additionally: Outdoor Lighting must have IP rating. Bulbs must have bulb type, lumens, color temperature, voltage and wattage. Ceiling, pendants, chandeliers, wall lights and table/floor lamps must have bulb type and bulb included/not included. Smart Lighting must have bulb type and voltage, with compatibility details (for example Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home) in the description. Other listed specs are optional when not applicable.
Q: Coupons and promotions (P1): which rules are needed — minimum spend, expiry date, usage limit, category restriction, first-time customers only? Can coupons be combined with sale prices or with each other?
A: Coupon rules we need: percentage or fixed amount discount, free-shipping coupon, minimum spend, expiry date, total usage limit and one-use-per-customer limit. Category restriction is nice to have but not required for launch. First-time-customer-only is not needed at launch. Only one coupon can be used per order, and coupons cannot be combined with each other. By default coupons do not apply to items already on sale; they apply to full-price items. Sale prices themselves are set on the product by admin.
Q: Notifications (P1): do promotional push notifications require explicit opt-in? Who creates and sends them? Is email confirmation required at launch, or only push?
A: Yes, promotional push notifications require explicit opt-in, and customers can turn them off. Order-related notifications (confirmation, payment, shipped, delivered, cancelled) do not need marketing opt-in. Our marketing/admin staff create and send promotional notifications from the admin dashboard or a simple push tool. Order confirmation email is required at launch as part of the P0 order confirmation, since guests and bank transfer customers rely on it. Push notifications are P1 and follow if time and budget allow.
Q: Reviews (P1): can customers edit or delete their reviews? Does every review need admin moderation before display?
A: Yes, every review is checked by admin before it is displayed. Only customers who bought the product can review it. Customers can edit their review, but an edit goes back into moderation. They can also delete their own review. Average rating and review count only include approved reviews.
Q: Platforms and launch: do iOS and Android launch at the same time? What is the target launch date and any budget or team limits that affect P0 scope? Which analytics tool and which day-one reports are required?
A: Yes, iOS and Android launch at the same time, ideally built from one cross-platform codebase to control cost. Target launch is end of February 2027. The budget is fixed and moderate, so P0 must be complete and working first, and any P1 feature that puts the date at risk moves to a post-launch release. For analytics we want Firebase/Google Analytics. Day-one reports needed are: product views, searches (including top searches and no-result searches), add to cart, remove from cart, wishlist additions, checkout started, orders completed, revenue and cart abandonment, with a simple conversion funnel. We need no custom dashboards at launch.
