# Product brief: build-a-flutter-e-commerce-mvp-for-smartphones-t

Build a Flutter e-commerce MVP for Smartphones. Target shoppers interested in this vertical. Example listing style: iPhone X ($899.99). MVP must include: product reviews and star ratings, shopping cart with quantity updates, order history, guest checkout and registered accounts. Mobile-first UX, small releasable increment, demo-ready in one sprint. The product should let smartphone shoppers browse, compare, and buy phones with confidence using social proof (reviews/ratings) and a simple checkout path—whether they create an account or check out as a guest—so we can validate demand and capture orders quickly.

## Target users
- Shoppers actively looking to buy smartphones on mobile
- Value-conscious buyers comparing models, prices, and reviews before purchase
- Returning customers who want saved accounts and order history
- Guest shoppers who prefer a fast checkout without signing up first

## Business goals
- Launch a demo-ready smartphone shopping MVP in one sprint
- Validate that customers will browse, add to cart, and complete orders on mobile
- Support both guest checkout and registered accounts to maximize conversion
- Build trust through product reviews and star ratings
- Enable repeat purchase via order history for registered users
- Keep scope to a small, releasable increment focused on the smartphones vertical

## Key features
- Product catalog for smartphones with listing style like iPhone X ($899.99) including name, price, and key details
- Product reviews and star ratings on product pages
- Shopping cart with quantity updates (add, increase, decrease, remove)
- Guest checkout without requiring an account
- Registered user accounts (sign up / sign in)
- Order history for registered customers
- Mobile-first Flutter UX optimized for smartphones
- End-to-end browse → cart → checkout → confirmation flow suitable for a one-sprint demo

## Constraints
- Flutter mobile app MVP only—small releasable increment, demo-ready in one sprint
- Vertical limited to smartphones for this release
- Must ship product reviews and star ratings, cart with quantity updates, order history, guest checkout, and registered accounts
- Mobile-first UX; desktop/web is out of scope for the MVP
- No complex multi-vendor marketplace, loyalty programs, or advanced personalization in this sprint
- Payments and fulfillment should be simple enough to demo (e.g., simulated or basic card payment and standard shipping assumptions)

## Open questions
- Which payment providers and methods (card only, Apple Pay / Google Pay, COD) are required for the demo?
- What is the initial catalog size and source of product data—manual seed list, CSV import, or admin entry?
- How should reviews be moderated—open posting after purchase only, or allow any signed-in user?
- Which markets, currencies, and tax/shipping rules apply for the first release?
- What account recovery and email/notification flows are required beyond basic sign-up and sign-in?
- Is inventory tracking and out-of-stock handling required in the MVP, or can stock be assumed available?
- What brand name, visual identity, and storefront naming should appear in the app beyond the working project title?
