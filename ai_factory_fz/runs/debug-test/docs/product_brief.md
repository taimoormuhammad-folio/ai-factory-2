# Product brief: Men's Shirts Shopping App

A fast, simple mobile app that makes it easy for men to find well-fitting, good-quality shirts for work, casual and occasion wear, and buy them in a few taps, with confidence about size and low hassle on returns. The app should become our main direct sales channel and build lasting customer loyalty to our brand.

## Target users
- Working professionals aged 25-45 who need smart and business-casual shirts and want to shop quickly from their phone
- Younger men aged 18-25 looking for affordable casual and trend-led shirts
- Men aged 45+ who prefer classic styles, reliable sizing and simple checkout
- Gift buyers (partners, family) purchasing shirts for men, who need help with sizing and easy returns/exchanges
- Internal store/operations staff who manage the catalog, stock, orders and returns through an admin back office

## Business goals
- Launch a first version (MVP) on iOS and Android within about 4-5 months of project start
- Move a meaningful share of sales to the app: target 25% of online revenue coming from the app within 12 months of launch
- Reach 50,000 app downloads and 10,000 monthly active users in the first year
- Achieve a mobile checkout conversion rate of at least 2.5%
- Keep the return rate below 15% by giving clear size guidance and accurate product info
- Build repeat purchase: 30% of customers buying again within 6 months, supported by accounts, wishlists and push notifications
- Reduce reliance on third-party marketplaces and their fees by driving direct sales

## Key features
- Product catalog browsing by category (formal, casual, linen, polo, t-shirts, etc.), fit, color, size, price and fabric, with search and filters
- Product detail pages with multiple photos, fabric and care details, fit description, size availability and customer reviews
- Size guide and fit helper (size chart plus a simple questionnaire recommending a size based on height, weight and preferred fit)
- Shopping cart and quick checkout with guest checkout option, plus sign-up via email, phone number, or Apple/Google login
- Payments through cards, Apple Pay/Google Pay and local wallet options, plus cash on delivery if supported in the launch market
- Customer account with order history, order tracking, saved addresses and saved payment methods
- Wishlist / favorites and back-in-stock alerts
- Promotions: discount codes, free-shipping threshold, and seasonal sale sections
- Easy returns and exchanges initiated inside the app, with clear return policy
- Push notifications for order updates, abandoned cart reminders and new arrivals (with user opt-in)
- Admin back office to manage products, inventory, prices, promotions, orders, returns and basic sales reports
- Basic analytics to track conversion, best-selling shirts, and drop-off points

## Constraints
- Budget for MVP is limited: roughly mid five-figure USD, so scope must be prioritized and extras kept for later phases
- Must work on both iOS and Android, ideally from a single cross-platform codebase to control cost
- Must integrate with our existing inventory/stock system so items shown are actually available
- Payments must be handled by a certified payment provider; we will not store card data, and the app must comply with relevant privacy and data-protection laws (e.g. GDPR/CCPA depending on market)
- Launch in one country and one currency and language (English) first, with multi-region support later
- Product photography and descriptions must be ready before launch, and we will supply the initial catalog of about 150-200 shirts
- Our small operations team must be able to run the back office without technical help
- App must load quickly on average mobile connections and meet standard accessibility practices

## Open questions
- Which country/countries and currencies will we launch in first, and which delivery partners and shipping rates will we use?
- Do we offer cash on delivery at launch, and if so in which areas?
- Will we use one payment provider or several, and which one gives the best fees for our market?
- Do we build the admin back office from scratch or use an existing ecommerce platform (e.g. Shopify) as the backend?
- What is the exact returns policy (window, who pays return shipping, refund vs. store credit)?
- Do we want a loyalty/rewards program in the first release or later?
- Should we offer made-to-measure or tailoring services at any point?
- Do we include in-app customer support (live chat) at launch, or only email/phone contact?
- What is the final budget ceiling and target launch date, including any seasonal deadline such as a holiday sale?
- Do we plan to add other products (trousers, accessories) after launch, and should the design allow for this?
- What brand identity assets (logo, colors, fonts) are final, and who supplies them?
