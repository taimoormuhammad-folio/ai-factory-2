# Product brief: ShopEasy Mobile App

A comprehensive ecommerce mobile app that lets our customers browse our full product range, buy in a few taps, and get their orders quickly and reliably, so that the app becomes our main sales channel and the easiest way to shop with us.

## Target users
- Existing in-store and online customers aged 18-55 who shop regularly and prefer buying from their phone
- New customers acquired through social media and paid advertising who want a fast, trustworthy checkout
- Repeat or loyalty customers who want to reorder favorites, track orders and receive offers
- Business staff (store managers, fulfilment and customer support team) who manage products, stock, orders, returns and promotions through an admin back office

## Business goals
- Launch a first version (MVP) on iOS and Android within about 6 months
- Make the app account for 30% of total online sales within 12 months of launch
- Reach 50,000 app downloads and 15,000 monthly active users in the first year
- Achieve a checkout conversion rate of at least 3% and keep cart abandonment below 70%
- Increase repeat purchase rate by 20% through loyalty rewards, reorder shortcuts and push notifications
- Reduce customer support load by giving customers self-service order tracking, returns and FAQs
- Collect first-party customer data (with consent) to improve marketing and inventory planning

## Key features
- User accounts: sign up and log in with email, phone number, Apple or Google; guest checkout; saved addresses and profile
- Product catalogue: categories, search with filters and sorting, product pages with photos, descriptions, sizes/variants, price, stock status and customer reviews
- Shopping cart and wishlist: add, edit and save items, with price and stock updated in real time
- Checkout and payments: credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and optionally cash on delivery, with promo codes and gift cards; secure payment handled by a third-party payment provider
- Delivery options: home delivery with standard and express shipping, and click-and-collect from our stores, with shipping cost and delivery date shown before payment
- Order management: order confirmation emails and push notifications, live order tracking, order history, easy reorder, and returns or refund requests in the app
- Loyalty and promotions: points for every purchase, personalized offers, discount codes, and seasonal campaigns and banners
- Push notifications: order updates, back-in-stock alerts, abandoned cart reminders and promotions, with user opt-in controls
- Customer support: in-app FAQ, contact form and live chat or email support
- Admin back office: manage products, prices, inventory, orders, customers, promotions, banners and basic sales reports
- Analytics and reporting: track sales, conversion funnel, popular products and campaign performance
- Integrations: connection to our existing inventory/stock system, payment gateway, shipping carriers and email/marketing tools

## Constraints
- Budget is limited, so the first release should focus on the essential shopping journey and leave advanced features for later phases
- Must work on both iOS and Android, ideally built from a single cross-platform codebase to control cost
- Must comply with data protection and privacy laws (such as GDPR), with clear consent for marketing and tracking
- Payments must be PCI-compliant by using a trusted payment provider; we will not store card details ourselves
- Product stock must stay in sync with our physical stores and warehouse to avoid overselling
- App must be fast and usable on mid-range phones and slower mobile connections
- Must follow our existing brand guidelines for logo, colours and tone
- Must meet basic accessibility standards (readable text, screen reader support)
- The team has no technical staff, so the solution must be easy for non-technical staff to operate without developer help

## Open questions
- Which countries and currencies will we sell in at launch, and do we need multiple languages?
- Which exact payment methods and payment provider should we use, and do we offer cash on delivery?
- Which shipping carriers and delivery pricing rules (free shipping threshold, delivery zones) will we use?
- What is the final budget and the firm launch date or deadline?
- Should the loyalty programme launch in the first version or in a later phase?
- What is the return and refund policy (time window, who pays return shipping)?
- Does our current inventory or ERP system have an API, and how will real-time stock sync work?
- Should we support marketplace or third-party sellers, or only our own products?
- Do we want a live chat with staff, or is email and FAQ support enough at launch?
- Who will maintain the app and handle product photography and content after launch?
- Which app name and branding assets will be used, and is the name available in the app stores?
- How will we handle tax, invoicing and VAT for different regions?
