# Product brief: build-a-flutter-e-commerce-mvp-for-laptops-targe

Build a Flutter e-commerce MVP for Laptops. Target shoppers interested in this vertical. Example listing style: New DELL XPS 13 9300 Laptop ($1499.99). MVP must include: shopping cart with quantity updates, guest checkout and registered accounts, promo codes at checkout, product catalog with search and filters. Mobile-first UX, small releasable increment, demo-ready in one sprint. We want a focused laptop storefront that helps shoppers find the right machine quickly, add it to cart, and check out with or without an account—so we can prove demand and start selling in a single sprint.

## Target users
- Shoppers interested in buying laptops (this vertical)
- Professionals and students comparing mid-to-premium models (e.g. New DELL XPS 13 9300 Laptop at $1499.99)
- Guest buyers who want a fast checkout without registering
- Registered customers who want an account for repeat purchases and saved details

## Business goals
- Launch a demo-ready Flutter e-commerce MVP for laptops in one sprint
- Convert browsing into orders via shopping cart, guest checkout, and registered accounts
- Drive conversion with promo codes at checkout
- Help shoppers discover products through catalog search and filters
- Validate the laptop retail offering with a small, releasable, mobile-first increment

## Key features
- Product catalog with search and filters
- Shopping cart with quantity updates
- Guest checkout
- Registered accounts
- Promo codes at checkout
- Mobile-first UX
- Example listing style: New DELL XPS 13 9300 Laptop ($1499.99)

## Constraints
- Flutter e-commerce MVP scoped to Laptops
- Mobile-first UX
- Small releasable increment
- Demo-ready in one sprint
- Must include shopping cart with quantity updates, guest checkout and registered accounts, promo codes at checkout, and product catalog with search and filters

## Open questions
- Which payment methods do we accept at launch (cards only, Apple Pay / Google Pay, PayPal)?
- Do we ship nationally only, or also internationally in the MVP?
- What is the return / warranty policy we show at checkout for laptops?
- How many SKUs and brands do we stock for the demo catalog beyond the DELL XPS 13 example?
- Who owns inventory truth for the MVP—manual stock, or a simple admin update?
- What promo code rules apply (percent off, fixed amount, first-order only, minimum spend)?
- Do registered accounts need email verification and password reset in sprint one?
- Is order history and order tracking required for the demo, or only confirmation after checkout?
