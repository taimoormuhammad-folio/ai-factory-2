# Clarifications

Q: Which payment methods do we accept at launch (cards only, Apple Pay / Google Pay, PayPal)?
A: At launch we accept major credit and debit cards only (Visa, Mastercard, Amex). Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal are out of scope for the first sprint; we can add wallet payments after we prove checkout conversion.
Q: Do we ship nationally only, or also internationally in the MVP?
A: National shipping only for the MVP. We will sell and deliver within our home country so we can keep shipping rates, tax, and fulfillment simple for a one-sprint demo.
Q: What is the return / warranty policy we show at checkout for laptops?
A: Show a clear policy at checkout: 14-day returns on unused laptops in original packaging; manufacturer warranty applies as stated on each product (typically 1 year for demo SKUs like the Dell XPS 13). Refunds go to the original payment method after inspection. Damaged-in-transit claims must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.
Q: How many SKUs and brands do we stock for the demo catalog beyond the DELL XPS 13 example?
A: Stock a focused demo catalog of about 12–15 laptop SKUs across 4 brands: Dell, Apple, HP, and Lenovo. Include the New DELL XPS 13 9300 Laptop at $1499.99 as the flagship example, plus a mix of student and professional mid-to-premium models so search and filters feel real without bloating the MVP.
Q: Who owns inventory truth for the MVP—manual stock, or a simple admin update?
A: Inventory truth is a simple manual stock count maintained by the business (product owner / ops), with a lightweight admin update to set quantity per SKU. No live warehouse integration in sprint one—stock is seeded for the demo and adjusted manually when we sell or restock.
Q: What promo code rules apply (percent off, fixed amount, first-order only, minimum spend)?
A: Support both percent-off and fixed-amount codes at checkout. For the demo seed: WELCOME10 = 10% off first order only; SAVE50 = $50 off with a $500 minimum spend; LAPTOP15 = 15% off any order with no minimum. One promo code per order; codes cannot be stacked.
Q: Do registered accounts need email verification and password reset in sprint one?
A: Password reset yes—registered customers must be able to recover access in sprint one. Full email verification before first login is not required for the MVP; collect email at signup and treat the account as usable immediately so guest-to-registered conversion stays frictionless. Verification can follow in a later release.
Q: Is order history and order tracking required for the demo, or only confirmation after checkout?
A: Only order confirmation after checkout is required for the demo—success screen plus confirmation email (or in-app receipt) with order summary. Full order history and live tracking are not required in sprint one; registered accounts can get a basic “your recent order” confirmation view if it is cheap to add, but deep history and carrier tracking stay out of scope.
