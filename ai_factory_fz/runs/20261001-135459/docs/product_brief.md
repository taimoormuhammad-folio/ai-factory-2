# Product brief: create-a-simple-ecommerce-app

Create a simple ecommerce app that lets customers browse products, add items to a cart, and complete purchases on mobile, so a growing retail business can sell online with a clear, easy shopping experience.

## Target users
- Everyday shoppers who want a quick, mobile-friendly way to browse and buy products
- Returning customers who expect saved preferences and a familiar checkout flow
- Retail staff or the business owner who need a simple way to manage catalog and orders without heavy technical complexity

## Business goals
- Launch a simple, reliable mobile shopping experience that converts browsers into buyers
- Increase online sales and reach customers beyond the physical store
- Keep the first version intentionally simple so it can ship quickly and be improved based on real customer feedback
- Build a foundation for catalog, cart, checkout, and order tracking that can grow with the business

## Key features
- Product catalog with clear product names, prices, images, and basic descriptions
- Search and simple category filters so customers can find products quickly
- Shopping cart to add, update quantities, and remove items
- Secure checkout with customer contact and delivery details
- Order confirmation and basic order status for the customer
- Simple account or guest checkout so first-time buyers are not blocked
- Basic inventory-aware product availability so customers are not sold out-of-stock items

## Constraints
- Keep scope to a simple ecommerce MVP — no complex marketplace, multi-vendor, or advanced personalization in v1
- Mobile-first experience is the priority
- Must be commercially realistic for a growing retail business: usable catalog, cart, and checkout before polish features
- Preserve the original brief intent exactly: create a simple ecommerce app
- Budget and timeline favor a lean first release over a full feature suite

## Open questions
- What product categories and approximate catalog size should the first release support?
- Which payment methods are required at launch (cards, digital wallets, cash on delivery)?
- Will fulfillment be local delivery, shipping, in-store pickup, or a mix?
- Is guest checkout enough for v1, or is customer login/account history required from day one?
- Are there branding, pricing, tax, or return-policy rules that must be enforced in the app?
- Which markets or languages should the app support initially?
