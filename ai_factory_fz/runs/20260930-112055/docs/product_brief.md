# Product brief: beauty-mvp

A simple, attractive mobile shopping app that lets beauty customers browse our products, view clear product details, and add items to a cart. This first version is a small demo that proves the core shopping experience and gives us something concrete to show stakeholders, before we invest in a full ecommerce platform.

## Target users
- Beauty shoppers aged 18-40 who buy skincare, makeup, and haircare products on their phones
- Existing customers of our retail business who want a faster way to browse the catalog than our current channels
- Internal stakeholders (owner, sales, and marketing team) who will review the first demo and give feedback

## Business goals
- Deliver a working first demo quickly, with a deliberately small scope of product browse, product detail, and cart
- Validate that customers find the browsing and cart experience easy and appealing before committing to a larger build
- Create a foundation in Flutter that can later grow into a full ecommerce app with checkout, accounts, and payments
- Collect stakeholder and early-user feedback to decide on the next phase of features and budget
- Showcase our product range with good imagery and clear pricing to build confidence in the brand's mobile presence

## Key features
- Product browse: a scrollable list or grid of beauty products showing image, name, price, and brand, with simple category filtering (for example skincare, makeup, haircare, fragrance)
- Product detail: a page showing larger images, name, price, description, size or shade options where applicable, and an Add to Cart button
- Cart: view added items, change quantities, remove items, and see a running subtotal
- Sample catalog: a small set of roughly 20-30 products loaded from a local or mock data source for the demo
- Built with Flutter so one codebase can run on both iOS and Android
- Basic, clean branded look and feel (logo, color palette, consistent typography)

## Constraints
- Scope must stay small: only browse, detail, and cart are in for the first demo
- No real checkout, payments, user accounts, or order processing in this version; the cart is for demonstration only
- Product data can be mock or static for the demo; no live inventory or backend integration required
- Must be built with Flutter and run on both iOS and Android devices or emulators
- Target timeline is a short build of a few weeks so the demo can be shown soon
- Budget is limited, so avoid custom integrations and complex infrastructure
- Product images and descriptions must use content we own or have rights to use

## Open questions
- Which platforms should the demo be shown on first: iOS, Android, or both?
- Should the cart persist between app launches, or is it fine for it to reset for the demo?
- Do we want search and sorting in the first demo, or only category filtering?
- What is the exact demo date and who are the stakeholders attending?
- Which products and how many should be in the sample catalog, and who supplies the images and copy?
- Should the demo show product reviews or ratings, or leave them for a later phase?
- Do we need a wishlist or favorites feature in a later phase?
- What backend or ecommerce platform (for example Shopify or a custom API) will we connect to after the demo?
- What payment providers and delivery options will we support in the full version?
- Do we need multi-currency or multi-language support, and in which markets do we launch?
- Are there brand guidelines (logo, colors, fonts) to follow, or should the team propose a simple style?
