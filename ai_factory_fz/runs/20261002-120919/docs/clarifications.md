# Clarifications

Q: Exact brand name and visual identity for the storefront
A: Brand name: SoftAura. Visual identity: clean, calm mobile storefront with soft sage green (#7A9E8E) as the primary accent, warm ivory backgrounds, charcoal text, and rounded product imagery—simple and trustworthy, not clinical or luxury-heavy.
Q: Whether we sell only our own skincare line or curated third-party brands in MVP
A: Curated third-party everyday skincare brands for the MVP (body wash, cleansers, moisturizers in the Olay Ultra Moisture Shea Butter Body Wash ~$12.99 style), so we can launch faster and validate demand before expanding into our own line.
Q: Shipping regions, rates, and free-shipping threshold
A: Ship to the contiguous United States only in this sprint. Flat rate $5.99 per order; free shipping on orders $35 and over. No international shipping in the MVP.
Q: Return/refund policy shown in the app
A: Show a simple policy in-app: unused, unopened items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method; opened/used products are not returnable except if damaged or wrong item received. Customers start a return from Order History.
Q: How many SKUs and categories to seed for the sprint demo
A: Seed 24 SKUs across 4 categories: Body Wash, Cleansers, Moisturizers, and Face Care. That is enough to browse, favorite, review, and checkout without bloating the one-sprint demo.
Q: Who can write reviews (verified buyers only or any signed-in user)
A: Verified buyers only—customers may leave a review and star rating after they have purchased that product, so ratings stay trustworthy.
Q: Promo code rules (percentage vs fixed amount, one code per order, stacking)
A: Support both percentage-off and fixed-amount codes. One promo code per order; no stacking. For the demo, seed WELCOME10 (10% off) and SAVE5 ($5 off), applied at checkout before tax and shipping.
Q: Push notifications and email receipts in or out of this sprint
A: Out of scope for push notifications this sprint. In scope: a simple order confirmation email receipt after a successful checkout (can be mocked/stubbed for the demo if full email delivery is not ready).
