# Product brief: Skin Care Flutter E-Commerce MVP

Build a Flutter e-commerce MVP for Skin Care. Target shoppers interested in this vertical. Example listing style: Olay Ultra Moisture Shea Butter Body Wash ($12.99). MVP must include: order history, promo codes at checkout, product reviews and star ratings, wishlist or favorites. Mobile-first UX, small releasable increment, demo-ready in one sprint. Give everyday skincare shoppers a simple mobile store where they can browse trusted body and face care products, save favorites, leave reviews, apply promos at checkout, and see past orders—so we can start selling and learn what customers want before a bigger build.

## Target users
- Shoppers interested in the Skin Care vertical
- Busy adults shopping for everyday body and face care (cleansers, body wash, moisturizers, similar to Olay Ultra Moisture Shea Butter Body Wash at $12.99)
- Repeat buyers who want order history, favorites, and promo savings on mobile

## Business goals
- Launch a demo-ready Skin Care mobile shopping MVP in one sprint
- Validate demand for a mobile-first skincare storefront with a small releasable increment
- Drive first purchases with promo codes at checkout
- Build trust through product reviews and star ratings
- Increase return visits via wishlist/favorites and order history

## Key features
- Flutter e-commerce MVP for Skin Care
- Product listings in the style of Olay Ultra Moisture Shea Butter Body Wash ($12.99)
- Order history
- Promo codes at checkout
- Product reviews and star ratings
- Wishlist or favorites
- Mobile-first UX

## Constraints
- Mobile-first UX
- Small releasable increment
- Demo-ready in one sprint
- Flutter as the delivery platform
- Scope limited to Skin Care vertical for the MVP

## Open questions
- Exact brand name and visual identity for the storefront
- Whether we sell only our own skincare line or curated third-party brands in MVP
- Guest checkout vs account required before purchase
- Payment methods for the demo (card only, wallet, or mock payment)
- Shipping regions, rates, and free-shipping threshold
- Return/refund policy shown in the app
- How many SKUs and categories to seed for the sprint demo
- Who can write reviews (verified buyers only or any signed-in user)
- Promo code rules (percentage vs fixed amount, one code per order, stacking)
- Push notifications and email receipts in or out of this sprint
