# Clarifications

**Q1. Where do the products come from?**
A: We will start with about twenty products that we enter ourselves. Customers can't add products.

**Q2. Do shoppers need an account?**
A: No. I want people to start shopping straight away. Their cart should still be there when they come back on the same phone.

**Q3. Do you need payments in the first release?**
A: Not yet. For now people only fill a cart and see what they would pay. Payments come later.

**Q4. Which currency and countries?**
A: US dollars only, and we only sell in the US for now.

**Q5. How fast should it feel?**
A: The product list should show up in about two seconds on a normal phone connection.

**Q6. Which phones?**
A: Both iPhone and Android.
