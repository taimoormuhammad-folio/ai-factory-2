# Product brief: RetailHub Mobile Shopping App

To enable our retail customers to browse, search, and purchase products anytime and anywhere through an intuitive mobile application that drives convenience and increases sales for our growing business.

## Target users
- Existing retail customers aged 18-65 who shop in our stores
- New customers discovering our products through app recommendations
- Mobile-first users who prefer shopping on their phones
- Repeat customers wanting faster checkout experiences

## Business goals
- Increase online sales by 40% within the first 12 months of launch
- Reduce cart abandonment by making the checkout process seamless
- Build customer loyalty through personalized product recommendations and offers
- Capture customer data to improve marketing and inventory decisions
- Reduce operational overhead by shifting some sales to digital channels
- Expand reach to customers who cannot visit physical stores

## Key features
- Product catalog with search and filtering by category, price, and ratings
- User account creation with saved addresses and payment methods
- Shopping cart with persistent storage
- Secure checkout with multiple payment options (credit card, digital wallets)
- Order tracking and history
- Customer reviews and ratings for products
- Push notifications for promotions and order updates
- Wishlist functionality for saving items for later purchase

## Constraints
- Initial budget supports development for iOS and Android with core features only
- Must integrate with our existing inventory management system
- App must work reliably on devices running iOS 13+ and Android 10+
- Cannot implement features requiring external partnerships without 2-3 month lead time for negotiation
- Must comply with standard PCI DSS requirements for payment processing
- Development must be completed within 6 months to capitalize on holiday shopping season

## Open questions
- What is our target daily active user count and monthly transaction volume for year one?
- Should we implement a loyalty points system in v1 or defer to a future update?
- Do we want to support guest checkout or require account creation?
- What payment methods are critical vs nice-to-have? Should we support only cards, or also digital wallets and buy-now-pay-later options?
- How do we handle returns and refunds - is this automated or manual review?
- Should the app include a live chat customer support feature or only email/phone support?
- What is our approach to shipping - do we offer free shipping thresholds, flat rates, or real-time carrier integration?
- How aggressive should our app promotion be - discounts only in-app, or matching store prices across all channels?
