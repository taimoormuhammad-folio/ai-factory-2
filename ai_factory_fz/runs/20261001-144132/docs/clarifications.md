# Clarifications

Q: Do we require user account creation for checkout, or should we support guest checkout for first-time buyers?
A: We should support guest checkout for first-time buyers. This reduces friction and lowers cart abandonment, which directly supports our goal to reduce abandonment rates. We'll still capture email for order tracking and future marketing. Account creation can be optional but incentivized with a faster checkout experience and access to order history and saved addresses for registered users.
Q: Which payment methods are mandatory for v1 (card only, or also digital wallets and buy-now-pay-later)?
A: For v1, we'll support credit and debit cards as mandatory, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay as digital wallet options since they're built into both iOS and Android natively and add minimal development burden. Buy-now-pay-later options like Klarna or Afterpay would require external partnerships with 2-3 month lead time, so we're deferring those to v2. Cards and digital wallets will cover the majority of our customer base.
Q: What is our returns and refunds policy - automated full refunds, or manual review process?
A: We'll implement a manual review process for returns and refunds in v1. This gives us control, protects against fraud, and integrates with our existing warehouse operations. Customers will initiate returns through the app with a reason and photos if applicable, which routes to our customer service team. Automated full refunds would be risky at launch without proper systems in place. We can automate this in v2 once we have better data and processes.
Q: Should v1 include customer support chat within the app, or only email/phone support?
A: For v1, we'll offer email and phone support only. Building in-app live chat would require additional infrastructure and support staffing that we're not ready for at launch. Email support through the app is fine, but live chat adds complexity. We'll evaluate adding chat in v2 once we understand our support volume and can staff it properly.
Q: What is our shipping strategy - free thresholds, flat rates, or real-time carrier integration?
A: We'll use a flat-rate shipping model for v1 with free shipping on orders over a certain threshold - let's say $50. This is simple to implement, easy for customers to understand, and encourages larger baskets. Real-time carrier integration is complex and would require negotiation with carriers, so we're deferring that. Flat rates also let us control costs while we ramp up volume. We can integrate real-time rates in a future release.
Q: Should the app display exclusive in-app pricing, or match store prices across all channels?
A: We'll match store prices across all channels. Our customers expect consistency and transparency, and channel-specific pricing creates confusion and erodes trust. If we want to run app-exclusive promotions, we'll do that through push notifications and the promotional offers feature - which feels like a special deal rather than hidden pricing. This approach is simpler operationally and better for our brand.
Q: Is a loyalty points system required for v1, or can we defer it to a future release?
A: We're deferring the loyalty points system to v2. While it's a great retention tool, it requires changes to our inventory system integration, accounting setup, and adds complexity to the checkout flow. For v1, we'll focus on the core shopping experience and use push notifications to drive repeat visits. We can launch loyalty in v2 once we have solid user traction and understand which rewards drive actual behavior change. This keeps us on schedule for the holiday season.
