# Clarifications

Q: Where will product data come from: an existing ecommerce platform (e.g., Shopify) or a new simple backend we build? Who will update products day to day and with what tool (spreadsheet, CMS, Shopify admin)?
A: We do not have an ecommerce platform we want to depend on for this release, so please use a simple managed backend or headless CMS (for example Firebase/Firestore with a simple admin, or a hosted CMS such as Airtable or Google Sheets feeding an API). Pick whichever is cheapest and easiest to maintain. Our catalogue is 50-150 products. Day to day, one or two non-technical store staff will update products (name, brand, price, description, ingredients, images, stock status, category). They are comfortable with spreadsheets and simple web forms, so the update tool must be a spreadsheet-style or simple form-based admin, with no code and no app release needed. If we move to Shopify later, the app's data layer should make that switch possible without a rebuild.
Q: What does the cart end in for the MVP: a 'Checkout' button that redirects to your website, an order request sent via email/WhatsApp, or a placeholder with no action? Note that our standard rules assume Stripe checkout, stock reservation and order statuses, but your brief puts checkout and payments out of scope, so please confirm these are excluded from this release.
A: Confirmed: real checkout, Stripe or any payment processing, stock reservation and order statuses are all excluded from this release. The cart ends in a clearly labelled 'Checkout' / 'Send order request' button that, for the MVP, sends the cart contents as a pre-filled message to us via WhatsApp (with email as a fallback) so a staff member can follow up manually. This also lets us measure real purchase intent. Nothing is charged in the app, and no orders are stored or tracked in-app. Stock is not reserved when items are added to the cart.
Q: Which market and currency will you launch in, and should the cart show tax or shipping estimates, or only a subtotal of item prices? (Prices will be stored as integer minor units with an ISO 4217 currency code.)
A: We launch in a single market, the United States, in English, with prices in USD. Storing prices as integer minor units with the ISO 4217 code USD is fine. The cart shows only line totals and an item subtotal, with no tax or shipping estimates. Add a short note in the cart such as 'Taxes and shipping calculated when your order is confirmed.'
Q: Do products need variants (shades, sizes/volumes) with separate prices, images and stock levels in the MVP? If yes, should stock status be shown per variant, and should out-of-stock variants be blocked from being added to the cart?
A: Yes, but keep it simple. The product detail page lets the customer pick a variant (shade or size/volume). Each variant can have its own price and its own stock status (In stock / Low stock / Out of stock), and it may optionally have its own image, though a shared set of product images is acceptable. We do not need exact stock counts, just a status that staff set manually. Out-of-stock variants are shown but greyed out, and the Add to cart button is disabled for them, so they cannot be added. The cart item records the chosen variant, so the same product in two shades appears as two separate lines.
Q: Which analytics tool do you prefer (e.g., Firebase Analytics, other), and do you need a consent prompt at first launch (e.g., for EU/UK/California users) or is the launch market one where a privacy policy disclosure is sufficient?
A: Use Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) because it is free, standard for Flutter and easy to set up. Track screen views, product views, search usage, and add-to-cart events, so we can see most viewed and most carted products. Since we launch in the US only, a privacy policy and in-app disclosure of analytics use is sufficient, and we do not need a blocking consent prompt at first launch. Please keep analytics anonymous, with no personal data, no ad tracking and no user identifiers beyond the default app instance ID. The design should allow a consent screen to be added later if we expand to the EU/UK. We will provide the privacy policy text, and you should link to it from the app and from the store listings.
Q: Who will supply the brand identity (logo, colors, fonts) and product photography, and will these be available before design starts? Are there any existing brand guidelines we must follow?
A: We will supply the logo, colour palette and fonts from our existing branding, and a short one-page brand sheet before design starts. We do not have extensive formal brand guidelines beyond that, so the designer should keep the look clean, modern and uncluttered. We will also supply product photography for all launch products, with at least one good image per product and 2-4 for our bestsellers. We will deliver them in batches, starting with the top 30 products in the first two weeks and the remainder before the content freeze. Images must be optimized by the app or backend, so please tell us the recommended image size and format, and we will export to that. Fonts should be free or commercially licensed for use in an app.
Q: Is the 'reorder favorites' need for existing customers in scope for the MVP? Without accounts or order history, should we only support the persisted on-device cart, or do you want a simple on-device favorites/saved list?
A: For the MVP we only need the persisted on-device cart, so customers can leave and come back to their items. A separate favorites or saved list is not in scope for this release, because it could grow the work and the brief limits us to browse and cart. Reordering is handled by customers searching for and adding items again. There will be no order history or accounts. A simple on-device favorites list is a good candidate for the next release together with accounts, and we will decide based on the analytics data.
Q: What is the target launch date and budget ceiling, and are there any minimum OS versions (iOS/Android) or device types (phone only vs. tablet) we must support?
A: The target is to launch in 10 weeks from project kickoff, within the 8-12 week window in the brief, with a hard stop at 12 weeks. That includes submission time for App Store and Google Play review. Our budget ceiling for the build is about $25,000 USD, plus low monthly running costs of under $100 for hosting, backend and services, and we would rather deliver less scope than exceed this. Supported devices are phones only, in portrait orientation. The app should run on tablets without breaking, but we will not design a tablet layout. Support the current and previous two major versions of iOS, and Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher, or whatever minimum Flutter's current stable release supports, as long as it covers the large majority of our customers' devices.
