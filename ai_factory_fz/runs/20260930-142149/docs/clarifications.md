# Clarifications

Q: Guest checkout: the brief lists guest browsing and guest checkout, but for the first release carts belong to signed-in users only (no guest carts). Can we confirm guests may browse, search and view products only, and must register or sign in to add to cart and check out? If guest checkout is required, it becomes a separate, larger scope item that includes guest order tracking.
A: Yes, confirmed. For the first release guests can browse, search, filter and view product details only. They must register or sign in to add to cart and check out. The brief mentions guest checkout, but I accept moving it, together with guest order tracking, to a later phase so we can launch sooner. Registration must be quick (email and password) and the app should prompt guests to sign up when they try to add to cart, without losing their place.
Q: Payment methods: the first release uses Stripe in test mode for card payments only (server creates the PaymentIntent, app confirms it, webhook marks the order paid). Are cash on delivery and bank transfer required in the MVP? If so, please describe each flow: who confirms payment, whether there is a maximum COD order value or COD regions, how long an unpaid bank-transfer order is held before auto-cancel, and how stock is handled meanwhile. Otherwise we will treat them as out of scope.
A: Card payments through Stripe are enough for the first release, and test mode is fine for development and acceptance. Cash on delivery and bank transfer are out of scope for the MVP and move to a later release. The brief lists them as business-required methods, so they must be planned for the next phase. When we add them, my intended rules are: staff manually confirm bank transfers and COD payment, COD is limited to a maximum order value that I will set then, and unpaid bank-transfer orders are held for 3 days before auto-cancel, with stock reserved during that time. Please design the order and payment data so these methods can be added without rework.
Q: Order statuses: the brief shows Pending, Confirmed, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, while our standard flow is pending_payment -> paid -> fulfilled -> delivered, plus cancelled and refunded. Do you accept the standard flow with customer-facing labels (for example, pending_payment shown as Pending, paid as Confirmed), or do you need Processing and Shipped as separate statuses that admins set manually? Either way, cancelled and refunded are required.
A: I need Processing and Shipped as separate statuses that admins set manually, because customers expect to see when an order is being prepared and when it has left us. The customer-facing flow is Pending (awaiting payment), Confirmed (payment received), Processing, Shipped, Delivered. Cancelled and Refunded are also required. Internally you can name them as you like as long as customers and admins see these labels. Customers should receive a notification when the status moves to Shipped and Delivered.
Q: Currency, shipping and tax: which single currency (ISO 4217) will we sell in at launch, and is multi-currency needed? Which countries or regions do we ship to, and how is shipping priced (flat rate, by region, by weight, free over a threshold)? Are displayed prices tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive, and is one tax rate enough for the MVP?
A: We sell in a single currency, USD, at launch. No multi-currency. We ship to the United States only (domestic, contiguous states) at launch. Shipping is a flat rate of $9.99 per order, and free on orders of $150 or more. Displayed prices are tax-exclusive, with tax calculated and shown in the cart and checkout. One flat sales tax rate, configurable by admin, is enough for the MVP. Region-specific tax rates can come later.
Q: Stock and unpaid orders: we plan to reserve stock when checkout starts (payment intent created) and release it if payment fails or expires, not when items are added to the cart. Do you agree? What payment timeout should apply (for example, 30 minutes), and what stock quantity counts as low stock (a global number or one per product)?
A: Agreed. Reserve stock when checkout starts (payment intent created), not when items are added to the cart, and release it if payment fails or expires. A 30-minute payment timeout is fine. Low stock is a global threshold of 5 units, applied per variant where variants have their own stock. A per-product override is nice to have later but not needed for the MVP.
Q: Cancellations, refunds and returns: can customers cancel their own orders, and up to which status? Are refunds admin-initiated only (full and/or partial) with no customer returns flow in the MVP? Please state any return window or policy that must be shown in the app.
A: Customers cannot cancel orders themselves in the MVP. They contact support, and admin staff cancel orders. Refunds are admin-initiated only, both full and partial, and there is no customer returns flow in the MVP. The app must show a simple policy page stating a 30-day return window for unused items in original packaging, with returns arranged by contacting support. Refunds go back to the original card.
Q: Platforms and admin: do we launch on both iOS and Android (with the admin area as a web tool), or one platform first? Which admin roles are needed in the MVP (a single admin role, or separate product manager, order staff and support roles with different permissions)?
A: We launch on both iOS and Android, ideally from a single cross-platform codebase, with the admin area as a web-based tool. For the MVP a single admin role with full access is enough. Separate roles for product manager, order staff and support can follow later, but please do not hard-code things in a way that blocks adding roles.
Q: Catalog setup: how many products, brands and variants will we launch with, and is a bulk import (for example, a CSV or spreadsheet) required in the MVP, or is manual entry in the admin area enough?
A: We plan to launch with about 300 products, 25 brands and roughly 800 variants across all the categories. Manual entry in the admin area is enough for the MVP. A CSV bulk import is not required for launch, though we would like it soon after, because our team will need to load the initial catalog quickly. If it is cheap to include a one-off import for the initial load, that is welcome but not a launch blocker.
Q: Account and language scope: is email and password sufficient for the MVP (no Google or Apple sign-in)? Which languages are needed at launch, and is account deletion with removal of personal data expected (our standard is yes)?
A: Email and password is sufficient for the MVP, with no Google or Apple sign-in. English is the only language at launch. Yes, account deletion with removal of personal data is expected. Order records needed for accounting and tax purposes may be kept in anonymized form.
Q: P1 rules that affect the data model: for coupons (percentage, fixed amount, free shipping), are the rules one coupon per order, combinable with sale prices, with expiry date, usage limit and minimum order value? Also, are promotional push notifications and analytics tracking subject to opt-in consent in your markets (for example, GDPR), and which analytics tool should be used?
A: Yes to all the coupon rules: one coupon per order, it can be combined with sale prices, and each coupon has an expiry date, a total usage limit and an optional minimum order value. Coupon types are percentage, fixed amount and free shipping. Promotional push notifications require explicit opt-in, and order-related notifications are sent as service messages. Analytics tracking should respect the user's consent choice, so please include a simple consent prompt. Although we launch only in the US, we want to be GDPR-ready for future expansion. For the analytics tool, use Firebase Analytics unless you recommend something cheaper and simpler. I will follow the delivery team's advice.
